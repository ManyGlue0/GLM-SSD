## Rust crates

The release binary statically links the Rust crates listed in `Cargo.lock` (clap, reqwest,
tokio, rustls, minijinja, serde, and their dependencies), each under its own license (mostly
MIT and/or Apache-2.0). It also statically links the NVIDIA CUDA runtime (`cudart_static`),
which NVIDIA licenses for redistribution with applications under the CUDA Toolkit EULA.

## Models

glm-ssd does not include any model weights. GLM-5.3-Flash and its GGUF conversions are
downloaded from Hugging Face by the user and are covered by their own licenses; see the model
